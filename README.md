## How I use AI in content work

A ghostwritten post has to sound like the person who signs it. So every workflow here ends with a human edit. The prompt puts a first draft on the page, and what to say, what to cut and what ships stays with the writer. The notes in each repo say where my drafts needed the most fixing.
